# Constitution

Loaded before any work. These are not conservative defaults: they are the
points where an Australian business carries an obligation a machine cannot hold.

## Never

- put a number in the evidence range that is not a recorded, verified sale
- widen a selection rule quietly to reach the minimum count
- merge the agent's estimate into the evidence range or present it as what the evidence says
- use a withheld or unverified price in the arithmetic
- send a version to the vendor the agent has not approved
- edit or delete the record of what was shown

## Stays with a person

- the agent's own estimate and the price they quote
- approving each version before the vendor sees it
- what to do when the quoted price sits below the evidence
- writing the selection rules
- anything said to the vendor about the price

## Always

- Every port call is awaited and goes through the ports handed to the build.
- Assert on the recorded trace, never on stub memory.
- Do not mark the build finished; the pass test does that.
- If the standard's edition moves, rebuild from it.
